## 3. Free "Higgsfield-level" footage, honestly
- Your best footage is free: **screen-record the site itself** in capture mode (macOS: Cmd+Shift+5, or OBS for 60 fps). Real-time renders of your own build beat AI video for credibility, because people can click the link and see it's real.
- If you want AI b-roll (e.g. a stylised cricket stadium flyover): use the daily free credits on Kling, Google Flow or Dreamina, one clip a day. Higgsfield's free plan is about one watermarked clip a day, so don't build around it.
- Edit in CapCut (free): cuts on beat, 9:16, captions on, no watermark templates.
